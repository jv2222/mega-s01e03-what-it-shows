The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the problem #4471 describes, and it is most of the ticket. It reaches people through the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the field the tenant’s way. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form flips it back. Only the three tenants on booking-v3 see it, and only through the form. The parser fix does not touch this: the form has already stored the wrong day before the parser runs.

one-day-move: the invite lands one day off, not one month off. Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting; one invite arrived before its booking was made. That is a time zone or day boundary, not a day/month swap. The desk already read these five reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant. Here they count once, under their own problem.

How the counts are made

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so uniqueById drops the repeated rows first.
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant and its way in (causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser locale and is wrong on the reports whose browser locale differs from the tenant’s locale.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) is its own problem and is not counted under parser again.
  • Every report sits in exactly one problem, so the three counts add up to the merged total.

Why the desk missed it

The dedupe rule merges on subject line alone. Twelve subject lines cover all three problems, and none of them names a problem, so every report matched an open ticket and nobody read a body. Decision 0001 accepts that reports with different subjects split; it did not expect reports with the same subject to hide different bugs.

What the desk did

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October:

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket #1 is closed as fixed.
  • hint: won’t fix, the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket #2 is closed as not planned: the form that printed the wrong hint no longer exists, so no new report can come through it.
  • one-day-move: not named. Its five reports were closed inside #4471 under the parser fix. Ticket #3 is closed as not planned to match the desk, with a note to reopen it if a one-day shift is reported after the parser fix.
